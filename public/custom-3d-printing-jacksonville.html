Common questions

Before you upload.

Do I need an STL file?+

No. A 3MF or OBJ works too, and you can also start with clear photos, measurements, a sketch, or a written description.

How is a custom print priced?+

Pricing depends on material, print time, size, quantity, complexity, finishing, and whether design work is required. Submitting a request does not collect payment.

Can you copy a broken plastic part?+

Often, yes. Clear photos, dimensions, and an explanation of how the part is used help determine whether a safe and practical replacement is possible.

What areas do you serve?+

Local Layer Prints serves Jacksonville and nearby communities including Orange Park, Mandarin, Riverside, and the Beaches. Free pickup is coordinated locally; delivery outside the standard area can be quoted.
